Why Standard ML & Time-Series Fail on Industrial Spare Parts

In aerospace, defence, and high-value manufacturing, spare parts do not follow smooth Gaussian trends. Instead, items sit dormant for 12 to 24 consecutive months before a maintenance overhaul triggers sudden, lumpy demand.

The Machine Learning Trap

Generic regression trees and neural networks trained with squared-error (L2) loss attempt to fit 90% zeros. They predict small positive fractional numbers (0.3 to 0.8 units) every single month. Over 5,000 SKUs, this accumulates massive phantom demand, inflating order levels and tying up millions in dead working capital.

The Naive Zero Paradox

Always predicting zero produces an appealingly low error on paper (median sMAE = 1.0000). However, predicting zero means ordering zero stock, guaranteeing 100% stockouts and aircraft grounding the moment an actual replacement part is needed.

CAFR Workflow: Detection → Attribution → Targeted Repair → Decision Cost

Rather than retraining large models indiscriminately or accepting passive forecast errors, CAFR introduces a causal diagnostic layer that identifies why a model is failing and applies closed-form policy remedies.

STEP 1

Detection

Continuous monitoring over rolling tracking signals, cusum boundaries, and residual variance jumps.

STEP 2

Cause Attribution

Classifies failures into a 7-cause diagnostic taxonomy (C1: Intermittency burst, C3: Obsolescence, C6: Ramp shift, etc.).

STEP 3

Targeted Repair

Executes matched counterfactual actions (R1: Damped intercept, R3: Obsolescence dampener, R6: Step reset) without retraining.

STEP 4

Inventory Evaluation

Measures outcomes in realised supply-chain currency: Cycle Service Level (α), fill rate, and total holding/shortage cost.

Core Methodological Insight

Accuracy is not service. Minimizing traditional statistical forecast error (MAE / RMSE) frequently produces inferior inventory decisions. CAFR connects causal forecast diagnosis directly to order-up-to stocking levels ($S_t$).

Strict Scientific Boundary: Why RAF Is an External Stress Test

Historical maintenance logs record requested demand, but do not record ground-truth causal failure labels ($C_0$–$C_7$) nor inventory holding/penalty costs. Therefore, RAF serves as an out-of-sample forecasting stress test. It does not validate CAFR's cause attribution or repair mechanisms (which require ground-truth labels).

Key Finding: SBA vs. LightGBM

Global LightGBM has 2.99× higher error than SBA (sMAE 12.7423 vs 4.2642). The degradation is statistically significant (paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test $p = 2.57 \times 10^{-58}$).

L2 tree loss attempts to fit 90% zero cells by predicting tiny positive decimals, accumulating +3,179 units of phantom drift.

Understanding the Zero Baseline

The Zero baseline achieves a lower mean sMAE (2.6697) and median 1.0000 because median demand in evaluation exactly equals burn-in demand.

However, Zero has severe negative bias (-1.4430) and 100% WAPE. In supply chains, predicting zero causes fatal stockouts.
